SLIDE 06 • CAPABILITIES
KEY SYSTEM FEATURES
7 purpose-built capabilities engineered for public safety and tactical command
1. Anonymous Crowd Intelligence
Detect crowd density and flow vectors without facial recognition, identity tracking, or biometric storage.
2. Venue-Aware Risk Mapping
Understand zones, gates, corridors, and structural chokepoints directly mapped to physical blueprints.
3. Risk Trajectory & Trend
Compute 1st derivative slope (dR/dt) to forecast whether danger is stable, increasing, or surging.
4. Critical-Zone Detection
Pinpoint the exact geometric zone experiencing acute pressure before spillover occurs.
5. Intervention Simulator
Virtually test candidate tactical actions (gate throttle, exit release, rerouting) in real time.
6. Feasible Action Recommendation
Select the safest viable action based on risk reduction, egress headroom, and zero downstream overload.
7. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard
AI acts strictly as a decision-support copilot; authorized human operators retain final operational authority with comprehensive audit logging.
